Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about Text Refiner? We have answers. Explore the inner details of our humanizing engines.

What is Text Refiner?

Text Refiner is a tool that helps make AI-generated or copied content appear more natural and unique by subtly altering its structure using invisible encoding.

How does the Humanizer work?

The Humanizer refines AI-generated content to make it appear more natural and unique.

What about the Plagiarism Remover?

The Plagiarism Remover performs a soft rewrite by changing phrasing, using synonyms, and restructuring sentences to improve originality and readability.

Is my text data stored?

No, all processing happens locally in your browser. Your text is not sent to any server or stored anywhere.

What file formats are supported?

You can upload .txt and .docx files, and download processed results in the same formats.

Can I use this for academic purposes?

While the tool can help refine text, we encourage ethical use. Always cite sources properly and avoid using it to misrepresent work as original.
