Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

Text Refiner processes text data locally in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or user content to external servers. Your intellectual assets remain entirely yours.

2. How We Use Your Information

All text processing, including humanization, synonym matching, and character-level encoding, occurs entirely on your device. No data is sent over the internet, ensuring complete privacy from data miners.

3. Cookies and Tracking

This website uses minimal local storage to retain your light/dark theme preference and auto-save the Forensic Auto-Typist timeline state. We do not use third-party tracking, profiling, or analytics tools.

4. Third-Party Links

The site may contain links to external sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or structural integrity of those external systems.

5. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. Because we do not collect any personal data, we do not knowingly process data from children under any jurisdiction.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally to support new offline browser capabilities. Updates will be highlighted here on this page.

7. Contact Us
